Shakil Hosen is a Bangladeshi Wikimedian and community leader actively contributing to the Wikimedia movement. He primarily works on Bangla Wikipedia, where he serves as an administrator and oversighter, and also contributes as an administrator on Bangla Wikibooks.

Beyond on-wiki contributions, he plays a key role in community development as the Deputy Coordinator of the Dhaka Wikimedia Community, organizing edit-a-thons, workshops, and outreach programs to engage new contributors. He has been involved in major events such as Wikimania Bangladesh 2022, Bangla WikiConference 2024, and various Wikimedia campaigns.

He was elected as an Executive Committee member of Wikimedia Bangladesh in 2026 and currently serves as a Treasurer (office bearer) for the 2026–2027 term.

He is also a technical contributor, developing tools and automation systems (including Telegram bots and Drive-to-Commons workflows) to support Wikimedia projects. His interests include open knowledge, community building, and the intersection of technology with collaborative platforms.
